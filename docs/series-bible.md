# Series Bible

**Series title:** Money Tails. Brand, opener, music and type proposals live in `docs/opening-sequence.md` until locked.

## Curriculum (teaching order, Australia-adapted)

### Foundations
- Needs vs. wants, delayed gratification
- How money is earned, income vs. expenses
- Compound interest (works for you saving, against you in debt)
- Inflation and why cash loses value

### Budgeting & saving
- 50/30/20 rule, zero-based budgeting
- Emergency fund ($1,000 starter, then 3 months of expenses) — **EP01, done**
- Pay yourself first / automatic transfers — **EP01, done**
- Transaction accounts vs. savings accounts, high-interest savers with bonus conditions
- Lifestyle inflation
- Sinking funds for big purchases
- Payment rails: PayID, Osko, BPAY

### Debt
- Credit cards: interest, minimum payments, the trap — **EP02, teased**
- Credit scores: Equifax/Experian, comprehensive credit reporting, BNPL reporting
- BNPL (Afterpay, Zip) ties up future paydays — seeded in EP01
- Good debt vs. bad debt (mortgage vs. payday loan/BNPL)
- HECS-HELP: indexation, repayment thresholds, pay-early-or-not
- Snowball vs. avalanche payoff
- Loans: APR/comparison rates, terms, amortisation, cosigning
- Renting vs. buying; rentvesting

### Investing
- Stocks, bonds, index funds, ETFs (ASX; generic examples like broad-market ETFs, no product recommendations)
- CHESS sponsorship, micro-investing apps (category level)
- Diversification
- Dollar cost averaging vs. market timing
- Bull vs. bear markets, volatility, staying the course
- Risk tolerance and time horizon
- Fees and expense ratios
- Superannuation: employer guarantee, salary sacrifice, concessional vs. non-concessional caps, choosing/consolidating funds, fees
- Property: offset accounts, redraw, fixed vs. variable, LMI, First Home Guarantee, First Home Super Saver Scheme, stamp duty, negative gearing
- Franking credits, CGT discount after 12 months
- Real estate, dividends, REITs

### Earning & protecting
- Negotiating salary, side income
- Tax: PAYG, marginal brackets, tax-free threshold, Medicare levy and surcharge, deductions
- Insurance: private health vs. Medicare, Lifetime Health Cover loading, income protection and life cover inside super
- Scams, get-rich-quick schemes, FOMO (Scamwatch, AFCA, ASIC Moneysmart)
- Estate basics: wills, beneficiaries, super death benefit nominations

## Main cast

- **KANGA** (lead) — eastern grey kangaroo. Emergency fund in the pouch. Calm, dry humour, never preachy. Light green tee under an olive-green work vest, pouch glows gold. Approved design: assets/series/kanga/.
- **MAGGIE** — Australian magpie. Impulse buyer, swoops on shiny things. Mismatched bright sneakers, orange sling bag. Arc: BNPL → credit card episode → reformed saver.

## Audience layering rule
Every episode carries three layers, one or two lines each, never dumbed down:
- Kids: the story and the visual metaphor
- Young adults: product mechanics (BNPL, automation, apps)
- Adults: targets and strategy (3-month fund, high-interest saver, super)
