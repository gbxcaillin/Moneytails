# Money Tails: opening sequence, theme and brand brief

Working proposal for the series title sequence, theme music, typography and colour. Everything here is a recommendation to react to, not a locked decision. Locked items move to `docs/series-bible.md` once approved.

## 1. The job the opener has to do

- Introduce the three leads (Kanga, Maggie, Hopper) and the wombat as a "and friends" beat, in that order of importance.
- Plant the series brand device: the glowing gold pouch. The opener should end on it.
- Run 15 to 20 seconds for YouTube. Anything longer gets skipped. Also cut a 5 second bumper and a 3 second sonic logo for Shorts.
- Contain no spoken lesson. It is pure character and energy. The lesson lives in the episode.
- Work with the sound off. The title must land as a picture.

## 2. Three visual concepts

### Concept A: The Coin Trail (recommended)

One continuous camera move along the main street toward the beach, with each character joining the run as the camera passes them. Coins are the connective tissue: every character interacts with a coin in a way that says who they are.

| Time | Beat | What we see |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 to 1.5 s | Sting | Black. One gold coin drops into frame, lands with the pouch chime, and lights up a pouch silhouette (storyboard panel 2 art). |
| 1.5 to 4.5 s | Kanga | The glow pulls back to reveal it is Kanga's pouch. She is on the main street, pats the pouch, gives the camera a knowing half-smile, and hops off. Camera tracks left to right with her. |
| 4.5 to 7.5 s | Maggie | A shadow crosses the sun. Maggie swoops in from top of frame chasing a spinning coin, snatches it mid-air, sneakers dangling, and lands running beside Kanga, already talking. |
| 7.5 to 10.5 s | Hopper | Hopper is on the bench strumming. A coin bounces off his hat. He looks up, shrugs, flicks the coin into the air with the guitar neck, and springs into the run with one big grasshopper leap. |
| 10.5 to 13.0 s | Wombat and cameos | Camera passes the roadside. The wombat mechanic catches the coin in his cap without looking up. In the far background the bear and bull are shoving each other over a newspaper (running gag seed). |
| 13.0 to 16.0 s | The leap | The three leads reach the end of the street and leap toward the beach and camera. Coins trail behind them and stream down into a pouch shape at centre. |
| 16.0 to 19.0 s | Title | The coins settle and become the MONEY TAILS wordmark. The pouch glow pulses once with the chime. Hold 1.5 s. |
| 19.0 to 20.0 s | Out | Hard cut to the episode cold open. |

Why this one: a single camera move is the cheapest thing to keep consistent across AI-generated clips, every character gets a two-second personality beat, and the coin motif connects directly to the pouch brand device.

### Concept B: The Pay Packet

The reusable "pay packet sliced up" animation from the episode. A pay packet slides in, gets sliced into four colourful segments, and each slice flips over to reveal a character portrait (Kanga, Maggie, Hopper, wombat). The first gold slice drops into the pouch and the pouch glow forms the title.

Strengths: it is the series' core teaching image, so the brand and the lesson are the same picture. Very cheap to make as it is mostly flat graphics. Weakness: characters are static portraits, so there is less energy and less charm than Concept A. Best used as the 5 second bumper rather than the full opener.

### Concept C: The Storybook

A pop-up book opens on a cream background. Each page turn pops up a character in a paper-cut version of their environment. The last page pops up the town, the characters wave, and the book closes to show the title on the cover.

Strengths: distinctive, and the "storybook" framing supports the fable tone. Weakness: paper-cut is a different render style from the flat-cel episode look, so it needs its own asset pass and would fight the style string. Park it unless the series wants a deliberately different opener texture.

## 3. Title treatment and logo (direction chosen)

Wordmark, not a symbol. Kids read "MONEY TAILS" once and remember it.

- **Lettering:** chunky, rounded, slightly bouncy baseline. Two lines, MONEY over TAILS, so it fits both 16:9 and 9:16. Gold letters with an orange bottom shade and the thick dark outline used everywhere else in the show.
- **Coin O:** the O in MONEY is a gold coin. This is the only letter that is not a plain letter. The S stays a normal S.
- **Three tails:** below the wordmark, three tails poke up from the bottom edge like a picket fence and overlap the base of TAILS: kangaroo tail on the left, magpie tail feathers in the middle, fluffy koala tail tuft on the right. The tails are the visual pun; they say "tails" without touching the letters.
- **Glow:** the wordmark always sits on the soft gold pouch glow, on every background, including thumbnails.
- **Backgrounds:** beach and sky for the opener title hit and channel art, plain cream with a faint sunburst for the end card and thumbnails.
- **Lockups:** primary (two-line with tails and glow), horizontal one-line without tails for the channel banner, and an icon-only version (the coin O alone, or the three tails alone) for the channel avatar and watermark.

Rendered direction: `assets/series/brand/logo-v2-beach.png` and `logo-v2-cream.png`. The earlier concepts A, B and C are kept in the same folder for the record. Everything here is direction for a vector logo, not the final artwork.

Notes for the vector pass:
- Draw the kangaroo tail smooth and thick, as in the beach version. The cream render gave it segment lines that read like a horn.
- Keep the koala tail as a round grey fluff tuft. Real koalas have almost no tail, so the tuft is a deliberate cartoon exaggeration and it should stay small and round so it does not read as a rabbit or a sheep.
- The three tails imply three characters. Kanga and Maggie own the first two. The koala tail introduces a koala who is not yet in the cast roster. Recommendation: cast the koala as the wise mentor for the adult-level topics (super, tax), which the series bible currently leaves open as an "optional elephant". A sleepy koala who has seen every market cycle fits the tone better than an elephant in an Australian coastal town, and it makes the logo honest. Hopper stays a lead; he just is not on the logo, which is fine because the grasshopper has no tail to show.

## 4. Typography

All picks are Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License, free for commercial use on YouTube, thumbnails and merch, with no attribution needed.

| Use | Font | Weight | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wordmark base | Fredoka | Bold or SemiBold | Rounded, chunky, friendly, reads at thumbnail size. Customise the O and S as above. |
| Wordmark alternative | Baloo 2 | ExtraBold | Slightly more playful and heavier. Choose this if Fredoka feels too corporate. |
| On-screen labels and numbers ($640, $40 per week, account names) | Nunito | Bold for numbers, SemiBold for labels | Rounded sans that matches the outline style, and its numerals are clear and evenly spaced, which matters because the show's numbers are the point. |
| End card three lines | Nunito | ExtraBold for the rule, Bold for the other two | Same family as labels so the end card feels like the show, not a slide. |
| Captions and subtitles | Nunito | SemiBold, with a dark outline or soft shadow | Legible over bright saturated backgrounds. |

Rules: never more than two type families on screen. Numbers always in Nunito Bold, always with a dark outline. No thin weights anywhere; the show's lines are thick and its type should be too.

Rule: gold means savings and nothing else. Do not use gold for debt, prices or warnings, so the colour keeps its meaning across the series.

## 6. Theme music

Direction: sunny, coastal, unhurried, with a hook a six year old can hum. Think ukulele, whistling, hand claps and a glockenspiel. Not corporate, not "finance". Nothing that sounds like a bank ad.

- **Tempo and key:** 118 to 124 BPM, C major or G major. Bright and easy to sing.
- **Instrumentation:** ukulele strum (the bed), upright or acoustic bass, hand claps and a shaker on the off-beat, glockenspiel for the coin motif, whistled melody, a light kick drum. Optional: a "boing" spring sample on Hopper's leap and Kanga's hops, kept subtle.
- **The coin motif (sonic logo):** a three-note ascending glockenspiel figure, for example E, G, C, ending on a short bright chime with a touch of shimmer. This is the sound of a coin landing in the pouch. It plays at 0 s, at the title hit, and again on the end card of every episode. Over time it becomes the show's audio brand on its own, and it is the 3 second sonic logo for Shorts.
- **Structure for the 20 second cut:** 0 to 1.5 s coin sting, 1.5 to 13 s ukulele verse building instrument by instrument as each character joins (ukulele alone for Kanga, add claps for Maggie, add bass and whistle for Hopper, full band by the wombat), 13 to 16 s a rising run under the leap, 16 to 19 s the title hit with the coin motif on top, 19 to 20 s a single ukulele chord tag.
- **Vocal hook, optional:** a small group of kids singing "Mo-ney Tails!" on the title hit, nothing more. Two words, sung once. If it feels twee, keep it instrumental.
- **Cut-downs to commission at the same time:** 20 s full, 5 s bumper (coin sting plus title hit), 3 s sonic logo, 30 s loopable bed for explainer segments (same palette, no melody), and a soft piano variation of the motif for Act 2 "the hit" scenes.

Prompt for a music generator, if you want a scratch track before commissioning a composer:

```
Upbeat children's TV theme, 120 BPM, C major, ukulele strumming, hand claps, shaker, upright bass, whistled melody, glockenspiel three-note ascending hook, sunny Australian beach town feel, playful and warm, no vocals, 20 seconds, ends on a bright chime
```

## 7. Sound design for the opener

- Coin drop and chime at 0 s and on the title.
- Kanga hop: soft double thump, no cartoon boing.
- Maggie swoop: wing whoosh plus a short excited squawk.
- Hopper leap: one spring "boing", the only one allowed.
- Wombat catch: a dull "tunk" into the cap.
- The leap: whoosh plus rising coin shimmer as the coins stream into the pouch.
- Keep sound effects sparse under the music. The music carries the opener.

## 9. How to produce it on OpenArt

- **Character beats (Kanga hop, Maggie swoop, Hopper leap, wombat catch, the group leap):** element-to-video on Kling 3 Omni or Seedance 2.0 with the approved sheets attached, 3 to 5 seconds each, one camera move per clip (continuous left-to-right track), same main street plate as the environment reference so the clips cut together as one move.
- **Coin sting and title hit:** do NOT ask a video model to form letters from coins; text morphing in video is unreliable. Generate the title card as a still (Nano Banana Pro handles in-image text well, see the brand concepts), then animate the coin stream and the glow pulse in the editor, or with image-to-video from a coins-only frame and composite the wordmark on top.
- **Assembly:** cut the five character clips end to end with the camera move continuous, overlay coin particles in the editor, land on the title card, add the music and the coin chime. Export 1080p 16:9, then reframe for 9:16.
