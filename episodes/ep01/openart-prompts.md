## Credit-saving tips

- Generate the explainer panels (14–19) as stills and animate only the diagrams. Character idle motion is optional there.
- The verandah three-shot (panels 13 and 26) is the hardest to get consistent. Generate it as three alternating close-ups if the wide keeps drifting.
- Reuse SS-3 cut 2 and SS-5 cut 1 from the same base generation; only the sky and Hopper's expression change.
- Never regenerate a whole Smart Shot sequence for one bad cut. Regenerate the single cut.
